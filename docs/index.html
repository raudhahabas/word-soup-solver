Word Soup Solver

In Word Soup you trace words through a 12×9 grid of letters. Played letters vanish and the rest fall down and to the right, so every word changes which words are left. Clear the whole board for a +500 bonus. I built five AI players with increasing lookahead. Watch them play, or try to beat them yourself.

      How the players work

      Finding words fast

      A depth-first search traces paths from every tile. A trie (prefix tree) of about 170,000 words cuts off a path the moment its letters stop being the start of a real word, so the search skips almost all dead ends.

      Greedy n-ply

      Greedy-1 takes the best-scoring word right now. Greedy-2 and Greedy-3 look 2 or 3 moves ahead (top 20 words per step) and favour lines that clear the board. Greedy-3 is ~280× slower than Greedy-1 but clears only a few more boards.

      Hybrid rollout

      Shortlist the 20 best moves, then play each one out to the end with a fast greedy policy and keep the line that clears the board with the most points. It looks deeper as the board empties. Result: 87% of boards cleared, compared with 6–10% for the greedy players.
